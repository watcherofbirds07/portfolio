My Music

I taught myself a lot of music theory during the pandemic, and have been actively composing since 2021. Although I have attempted to write music in many styles, the genres I always come back to are:

  • (Neo)Classical,
  • Jazz
  • Indie/Indie Folk
  • Alternative Rock (à la Neutral Milk Hotel), and
  • Video Game Soundtracks.

Songwriting - as in writing music with lyrics - is something of a new development for me, and as such I haven't released any songs to my Spotify yet. My Spotify, which is linked in below in the footer, showcases my work in classical, jazz, and video game music. I am currently working on a soundtrack for an indie game concept which you can listen to through the playlist titled "WILT", which is on my Spotify.
