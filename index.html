Who Am I?

Hello! My name is Colten Carlson, and I'm a PSEO student at Saint Paul College. This semester, I am taking

  • Web Fundamentals/HTML,
  • Introduction to Watercolor, and
  • Introduction to 2D Design

I have a background in music composition and performance, and post my compositions on Spotify, YouTube, and MuseScore under my name (Colten Carlson), though by the time you're reading this I've probably changed my handle to "Parsley and Thyme". Music - and storytelling through music - has always been my passion, and it's what I'd like to do for the rest of my life if possible. I am a multi-instrumentalist and play piano, guitar, trombone, ocarina, and I'm currently learning how to play (alto) saxophone! Beyond music, I am very interested in writing, and am currently working on a novel set in Duluth. As you've probably gathered by now, most of my experience falls under the umbrella of "the arts", so this class has been a challenging, but interesting change of pace for me.
